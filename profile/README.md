<p align="center">
  Darkmatter is a dark theme for the open web, adapted from
  <a href="https://github.com/metalelf0/base16-black-metal-scheme">base16 Black Metal Bathory</a>.
  Sixteen colors, kept quiet so your code and your work are what stand out.
  It runs in your terminal, your editor, your shell and across the whole desktop.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Want it somewhere it isn't yet? <a href="https://darkmattertheme.com/contribute">Make a port</a>.
</p>
